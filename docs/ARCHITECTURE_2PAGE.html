What NIRIKSHAK does not claim — true at commit 28ab52b

  • Encryption at rest. Required by Rule 6; not built. The databases and uploads/ are plain files (DEF-21).
  • A chained record of each AI suggestion. Required by CLAUDE.md §9; ai_suggested is never appended (DEF-20).
  • ISO/IEC 27001. Not in the selector: a purchased standard, not held. STIG and CIS editions describe Cisco IOS XE 17.x only.
  • An official framework mapping. Every mapping is project_asserted.
  • Calibrated confidence or top-3 accuracy. No line-level ground truth; calibration refuses under 200 samples.
  • Absence-aware evaluation on real data. No pack ships a platform default or capability claim.
  • An exposure ranking. No pack declares management interfaces, so prioritisation abstains.
  • Held-out generalisation. PAN-OS is sealed; XML and JSON modes raise rather than parse.
  • Field accuracy. Every corpus file is hand-written by the team; labels are unreviewed.
  • A tamper-proof log. Tamper-evident only (ADR 0008).
  • Serial numbers. Not present in a configuration export (ADR 0044).
  • FAISS or any LLM. Ranking is an exact cosine scan (api/learn/suggest.py).
